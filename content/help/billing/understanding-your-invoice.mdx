## Understanding Your Invoice

### Invoice Overview

Each invoice includes:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Invoice Number** | Unique identifier for the invoice |
| **Billing Period** | The date range covered |
| **Services** | Itemized list of care services provided |
| **Hours** | Total hours of care during the period |
| **Rate** | Your hourly or package rate |
| **Total Due** | Amount to be paid |
| **Due Date** | Payment deadline |

### Making a Payment

1. Navigate to **Billing** in your family portal
2. Click **Pay Now** on the outstanding invoice
3. You'll be redirected to our secure Stripe payment page
4. Complete the payment with a credit card or bank transfer

### Payment Methods

We accept:

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- ACH bank transfers
- Monthly auto-pay (set up in Billing Settings)
