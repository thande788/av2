## Viewing Your Care Schedule

### For Family Members

1. Log in to your **Family Portal**
2. Click **Schedules** in the sidebar
3. You'll see a calendar view of all upcoming visits

Each visit card shows:

- **Caregiver name** and photo
- **Date and time** of the visit
- **Services** to be provided (e.g., personal care, companionship)
- **Status** (scheduled, in-progress, completed)

### Filtering & Searching

Use the date picker at the top to jump to a specific week or month. You can also filter by caregiver name if your loved one has multiple caregivers assigned.

### Past Visits

Switch to the **History** tab to review completed visits, including shift notes left by the caregiver.
