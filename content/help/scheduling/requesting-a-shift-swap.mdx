## Requesting a Shift Swap

If you can't make a scheduled shift, you can request a swap with an available coworker.

### How to Request a Swap

1. Go to your **Schedule** in the employee portal
2. Find the shift you need to swap
3. Click the **Request Swap** button
4. Select an available coworker from the list (or leave it open for anyone to pick up)
5. Add a brief reason for the swap
6. Submit the request

### What Happens Next

- Your request goes to the **admin team** for review
- The proposed replacement caregiver will be notified
- Once approved, both schedules update automatically
- You'll receive a confirmation notification

### Important Notes

- Swap requests must be submitted at least **24 hours** before the shift
- Emergency swaps can be requested at any time but require admin approval
- You cannot swap with a caregiver who doesn't have the required skills for the client
