## Welcome to Your Family Portal

The family portal gives you full visibility into the care your loved one receives. Here's what you can do:

### View Care Schedules

The **Schedules** tab shows all upcoming and past visits. Each entry includes the caregiver's name, visit date/time, and the services provided.

### Track Invoices & Payments

Navigate to **Billing** to view invoices, check payment history, and make payments through our secure payment portal.

### Communicate with Your Care Team

Use the **Messages** section to send notes to your assigned caregivers or to the Angel Touch office.

### Submit Satisfaction Surveys

After each visit, you'll have the option to rate the service and leave feedback. Your input helps us maintain the highest quality of care.
