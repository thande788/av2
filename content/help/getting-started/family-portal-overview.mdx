## Welcome to Your Family Portal

The family portal gives you full visibility into the care your loved one receives. Here's what you can do:

### View Care Schedules

The **Schedules** tab shows all upcoming and past visits. Each entry includes the caregiver's name, visit date/time, and the services provided.

### Track Invoices & Payments

Navigate to **Invoices** to view invoices, check payment history, and make payments through our secure payment portal.

### Manage Your Account

At the bottom of the sidebar you'll find three icons:

- **Avatar** — Click your profile picture to manage your account. You can change your password, enable two-factor authentication, and view active sessions.
- **Theme toggle** — Switch between light and dark mode.
- **Sign out** — Log out of the portal.

You can also manage security settings from the **Settings** page under the Security section.

### Submit Satisfaction Surveys

After each visit, you'll have the option to rate the service and leave feedback. Your input helps us maintain the highest quality of care.
