## Your First Login

Welcome to Angel Touch Homecare! Here's how to get started after your first login.

### Complete Your Profile

1. Click on **Profile** in the sidebar
2. Upload a professional photo
3. Verify your contact information
4. Add your certifications and skills

### Manage Your Account

At the bottom of the sidebar you'll find three icons:

- **Avatar** — Click your profile picture to open account settings. From there you can change your password, enable two-factor authentication, and manage active sessions.
- **Theme toggle** — Switch between light and dark mode.
- **Sign out** — Log out of the portal.

### Set Your Availability

Navigate to **Availability** and fill in the hours you're available to work each week. This helps schedulers assign you to the right shifts.

### Review Assigned Shifts

Check the **My Shifts** tab for any shifts already assigned to you. You can view client details and special care notes for each visit.

### Clock In & Out

When you arrive at a client's home, use the **Clock In** button on your dashboard. Don't forget to clock out when you leave — this ensures accurate timekeeping.

### Submit Shift Notes

After each visit, write a brief note about the care you provided. These notes help the family stay informed and ensure continuity across caregivers.
